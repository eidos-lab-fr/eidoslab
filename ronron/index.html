A tiny Mac app

A cat that keeps you company while you work.

It sleeps at the bottom of your screen, wakes up when you type, and sits on your window when it is time for a break. Pair it with a friend, and your two cats work side by side.

Download for MacView the source

Free and open source · macOS 13 or later. The app is not notarized by Apple. The first time, right-click it in Applications, choose Open, then confirm.

What it does

It reacts to you

Asleep when you are away, awake when you type, excited when you type fast.

It asks for breaks

After 45 minutes of work it walks to your window, sits on it and waits. Pet it to send it home.

Choose your cat

Ginger, black, white, grey, calico or siamese. Give it a name.

Bring a friend

Share a code once. From then on, your friend's cat walks onto your screen whenever they are at their Mac.

They mirror you both

Side by side when you both work. Chasing a ball of yarn when you both type flat out. Curled up together when you both stop.

Say hello without a message

Click your friend's cat: hearts appear on their screen.

With a friend

Install Ronron

The cat settles in a corner of your screen. Drag it wherever you like.

Invite a friend

Menu → Friend → Invite a Friend. Send them the eight-character code.

Work together, apart

Their cat arrives when they do, and leaves when they leave.

Six coats, and what they do together

Private by design

Ronron asks for no permission and never sees what you type: it reads a count of key presses, not the keys. With a friend, your cat sends one of four words (away, resting, working, typing flat out), its name and its coat. These messages are encrypted on your Mac with a key derived from your code before they pass through a public relay.
